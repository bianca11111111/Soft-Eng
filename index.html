Archive Workflow Overview

From submission to long-term retention with clear accountability.

Step 1 — Intake & Submission Documents are submitted through designated user roles with required metadata fields and file validation.
Step 2 — Classification & Tagging Records are categorized by type, department, date, and operational tags for easier indexing and search.
Step 3 — Review & Decisioning Authorized reviewers validate content, set status decisions, and maintain traceable change history.
Step 4 — Archive & Access Approved records are retained securely and remain searchable, downloadable, and auditable.
  • Version-aware records lifecycle management
  • Soft delete and restore safeguards
  • Batch download and export-ready structure
  • Role-driven visibility and permission boundaries
  • Mobile-friendly access for field and office users
